## STEP 7 — NEVER post

- Internal financials, runway, cap table
- Confidential customer information
- TAM numbers (15K+ figure stays internal — for investor decks only)
- Sovereignty stance softening (don't accidentally write "we might consider AWS")
- Bad-mouthing competitors by name (subtle differentiation YES, attack NO)
- Politics, religion, divisive social issues
- Direct "buy MindSet" pitches (cringe + ineffective)
- LinkedIn content cross-posted unchanged
- Pricing model details
- Public "Boost10x please amplify" asks (do via WhatsApp, never on-timeline)

## Part 2 — Alumni investors / advisors

### Polytechnique Ventures — the strongest single lead in this whole doc
An **alumni-run VC fund**, created 2020 by X alumni **Denis Lucquin (X77, ex-managing partner of Sofinnova Partners)** and **Cécile Tharaud**. Capital comes from alumni (engineers, doctors, master's/exec-program grads), managed by Equitis Gestion (AMF-approved). First fund: €36M, 18 companies backed, 2 exits. Second fund: €21M closed so far, targeting €30-40M, 15-20 more deeptech pre-seed/seed startups.

**"Industry 4.0" is explicitly named as one of their target sectors** (alongside energy/climate, smart city/mobility, AI, medtech) — this isn't a generic "we invest in tech" fund, Industry 4.0 is one of maybe 5 named verticals. Eligibility: founder is an X alumnus (you are), OR tech spun off from Polytechnique labs, OR went through the school's incubator.

### X-Novation Center / X-UP incubator (École Polytechnique, on-campus)
Not a fund — an incubator, but with a named **"Industries of the future (Mobility, Industry 4.0, New Space)"** track, one of 4 program themes. Free program in exchange for a 5% equity stake at company creation. Includes access to X-FAB (50+ machines, prototyping/pre-industrialization space) and a mentor network across scientific/industrial/commercial domains. 120+ startups supported to date.
- Worth weighing the 5% equity cost against what you'd actually get (mentor network + prototyping space + alumni credibility) — this is a real trade-off to think through, not a pure "warm intro," so flagging it as a decision rather than a slam-dunk.
- Source: [École Polytechnique — X-UP incubator](https://www.polytechnique.edu/en/novationcenter/incubator), [École Polytechnique press release on X-UP relaunch](https://www.polytechnique.edu/en/press-room/press-releases/ecole-polytechniques-incubator-launches-new-x-offer-support-deeptech-entrepreneurship)

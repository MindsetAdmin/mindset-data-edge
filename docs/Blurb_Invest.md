The Problem: Industrial leaders face immense margin pressure, yet critical operational inefficiencies remain invisible. Their core systems (PLCs, MES, ERP, historians) operate in silos, each holding its own fragmented "version of the truth." Teams waste days manually reconciling Excel sheets, reacting to yesterday’s data instead of today's reality. In a mid-sized plant this "invisibility tax" is measurable: six-figure weekly losses to sub-minute stoppages the ERP never sees, and multi-day root-cause investigations for events that could be explained in three minutes. This operational disconnect creates a massive information latency tax, which directly drives decision latency, stalling modern AI strategies because agents lack the real-time, unified context required to reason and act.

The market: 15,000+ mid-sized European factories fit this profile — with an initial focus on pharma, cosmetics, agrifood, and metallurgy, where compliance and margin pressure make the pain sharpest.

The Vision: For decades, manufacturers have been trapped by rigid, multi-million-dollar software forced onto them through painful, multi-year IT overhauls. In the agentic era, chasing fragmented applications is a losing game; the infrastructure itself must be rethought. The companies that win the next decade will not buy more monolithic software. They will own their data layer to become autonomous, "reasoning enterprises", equipped to solve their own problems on their own terms.

Why now: three forces are converging — AI agents are moving from demo to deployment and hitting a wall on OT context; EU data-sovereignty regulation is closing the door on cloud-first industrial stacks; and margin compression on mid-market manufacturers is making "we'll integrate next year" a non-answer. The window for a European, sovereign, AI-native data layer is open now and will close.


The Solution: MindSet Data is the unifying, transversal layer built on top of existing factory setups, delivering a single source of truth without disrupting daily operations. No PLC changes, no new hardware to procure — the platform runs on any existing industrial PC or a small edge box we ship, and installs in under a day. Instead of parsing data after the fact, we structure raw machine signals and IT metrics into a live, central hub: a deterministic Knowledge Graph. This gives factory teams, legacy tools, and AI agents the trusted, real-time context needed to make optimal decisions in seconds. Your data foundation is not a prerequisite to the AI strategy. It is the AI strategy.

Why existing solutions fall short:
Every platform stops at clean, contextualized data and hands it off for someone else to act on. They tell you where the data is; none of them tells you which event to act on first, and why. MindSet Data crosses every event in real time with what makes it matter, the order book, the inventory, and the operational risk. We don't prioritize at random: we rank every event by its economic stake. They give you data. We give you the call to make.

Technology & Moat:
A native infrastructure layer that connects everything while changing nothing — no PLC modifications, no new hardware to procure, no data leaving the factory.

Connect: Production-ready connectors map both OT telemetry (OPC-UA, Modbus) and IT business context (ERP, MES, LIMS) into a single model — the bridge between shopfloor and office. A secure edge binary, installed in minutes, strictly read-only against the customer's IT systems, with no inbound ports exposed to the internet: raw data never leaves the factory network, no attack surface added.

Compute — pipelines: A no-code DataOps Studio where operations teams build event-driven data pipelines visually — drag OPC-UA source, transform, route to dashboard or downstream system — without waiting for IT.

Compute — Impact Engine: A deterministic engine that crosses every event with its business context, ranks it by economic stake (cost of lost margin, opportunity of a saved batch), and triggers the right action to the right person. Every figure is auditable line-by-line back to the source signal.

Expose: A native MCP layer exposes the Knowledge Graph securely, so any AI agent — today's or tomorrow's — can query factory operations safely, out of the box.

The Moat: The Knowledge Graph compounds with use, accumulating not just how the factory is built, but what each event costs — a proprietary economic map that gets more valuable the longer it runs. Hardware- and AI-agnostic by design, it keeps manufacturers free from vendor lock-in: their data foundation stays theirs, deployable on-premise, self-hosted, or hybrid — European data-residency by default.
